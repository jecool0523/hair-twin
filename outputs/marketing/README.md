# Hair Twin Marketing Outputs

This folder is the durable workspace for CMO artifacts.

- `research/`: market, competitor, buyer, patent, and trend research.
- `creative/`: mood boards, ad directions, generated visuals, brand assets.
- `campaigns/`: launch plans, acquisition experiments, ad copy, landing messaging.
- `customer-development/`: interview guides, surveys, validation notes, insight synthesis.
- `sales-enablement/`: pitch materials, ROI stories, objection handling, demo scripts.

Primary source: `PROJECT_PLAN.md`.

Current CMO stance: market Hair Twin as a premium salon consultation product, not a casual beauty-filter app.
